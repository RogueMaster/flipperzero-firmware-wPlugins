# Step Seq

A step sequencer for the Flipper's piezo speaker, with a melody grid and a drum row.

## Features

- **Melody:** one note per step across 15 pitch rows that follow the chosen scale, so everything stays in key
- **Note length, accents and chords:** notes can be short or held for up to 8 steps, accented, or played as a fast chiptune-style chord
- **Drums:** kick, snare, hi-hat, open hat, clap and tom made from square-wave sweeps and noise
- **Patterns:** eight slots of 16 or 32 steps, each with its own tempo, swing, scale, root and octave
- **Song:** chain the patterns into a song of up to 16 places
- **Scales:** minor and major pentatonic, minor, major, blues and chromatic, in any root
- **Tools:** transpose, shift, random melody and undo
- **Export:** writes the melody as a music file for the Music Player app
- **Saving:** all patterns and the song are saved to the SD card when you quit

## Controls

- **D-pad:** move the cursor; up/down also previews the pitch
- **Down on the drum row:** pick the next drum sound
- **OK:** place or remove a note or drum, or apply the chosen tool (accent, length, chord)
- **Back:** play / stop
- **Hold OK:** settings, tools, song editor and export
- **Hold Back:** save and quit
