App Search lists every app installed on your Flipper Zero, plus the built-in
tools, in one place and launches them. The most-used apps float to the top, so
your favorites are one press away.

Features
- One list of every installed .fap (scanned from /ext/apps, including
  sub-folders) and the built-in tools: Sub-GHz, NFC, Infrared, 125 kHz RFID,
  iButton, GPIO, Bad USB and U2F.
- Real app names, read from each app's own manifest on the device.
- Fuzzy search by name: press Left to open the keyboard and type.
- Most-used-first ranking: every launch is counted and remembered.
- Fast re-opens: scanned names are cached, so the list is ready in well under a
  second once it has been built, and only new or changed apps are re-read.
- Returns to App Search when the app you launched exits.

Controls
- Up/Down move the selection, OK launches the selected app.
- Left opens the search keyboard; type and confirm to filter the list.
- Back returns to the list from search, or exits from the list.
