# Yulia's Cats

A cozy virtual pet. Yulia shares a little room with her two cats: Nugget, an elderly tiger-striped gentleman with white socks, and Baby, a chonky grey cat with big bright eyes.

## Features

- **Look after the cats:** dinner and treats, yarn and a laser dot, and petting that makes the Flipper purr
- **Cats with habits:** left alone they watch birds, nap in the sun, climb the cat tree and follow each other around
- **Yulia:** she drinks tea, reads (with a line from a classic book on the page), draws portraits of the cats and plays her keyboard, and now and then says something to the cats in Russian
- **Wardrobe:** nine hair styles in three colours, nine choices of glasses and nine sweaters
- **Decor:** seven rugs, shelf plants, a cat tree and fairy lights
- **Vinyl:** optional background music in six genres, with a reaction from everyone when a new record goes on
- **Day and night:** the room follows the Flipper's clock, from sun through the window to a lamp-lit evening
- **Art:** when Yulia finishes a piece she holds it up full screen: portraits of the cats, abstract jokes after Malevich, Kandinsky and Mondrian, and studies that come out different every time, all kept in her sketchbook
- **Dreams:** at nap time Yulia drifts off and dreams, a little moving picture each time: her cats, travels with her tall curly-haired fellow, fantasy, space, surreal visions and the occasional nightmare, all written up in her dream journal
- **Album:** fifteen photos to unlock, including ones for reading every book, filling the sketchbook and filling the dream journal
- **Gentle:** time passes while the app is closed, but nothing bad ever happens to the cats

## Controls

- **Left / Right:** choose a group, or a choice inside a group
- **OK:** open the group, or do it
- **Back:** leave the group; from the top, save and quit
- **Up / Down:** show how everyone is doing

The music is off until you turn it on, and stays silent in stealth mode.
