Guitar Chords turns your Flipper Zero into a chord reference and a practice metronome.

Browse by root note, pick a chord, and read the fretboard diagram: muted and open strings, finger numbers, and a solid bar where a barre is needed. Most chords ship with two or three ways to play them, so you can pick the open shape or the movable barre that fits where your hand already is.

## Chord library

- 281 voicings across 99 chord names.
- All twelve root notes: C, C#, D, Eb, E, F, F#, G, Ab, A, Bb and B.
- Major, minor, 7, m7, maj7, sus2, sus4 and power chord shapes for every root.
- Left and Right cycle the voicings of the chord you are looking at.
- Up and Down jump to the next chord in the same root's list.

## Practice mode

Pick one of 14 built-in progressions and the Flipper becomes a metronome that shows you the chord to play. Progressions range from two-chord transition drills to four-chord standards like I-V-vi-IV, the 50s doo-wop turnaround, ii-V-I and a twelve bar blues.

Each chord lasts one bar of four beats. There is a click on every beat and a higher click plus a vibration on the downbeat where the chord changes, so you can keep your eyes on your hands instead of the screen.

- OK starts and pauses the metronome.
- Up and Down set the tempo, anywhere from 40 to 200 bpm.
- Left and Right step through the progression by hand.

## Bring your own chords

Both the chord library and the progression list live on the SD card as plain text files, one chord or one progression per line. Add your own, reorder them, or delete the ones you never use. Restart the app and the changes are live. No rebuild required.

The files are written to the apps_data folder for Guitar Chords the first time you run the app, so there is always a working example to edit.
