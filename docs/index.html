About

- The Eye — a beautiful animated radar-iris that sweeps while it watches. Calm when all is well; the pupil narrows and the screen frames itself in an alarm border under attack. - Deauth / disassoc detection — counts hostile management frames and measures their rate. A burst past your threshold = a deauth storm, and Argus screams (sound + vibe + red LED). - Evil-twin detection — set your network as the Guarded SSID; Argus flags any other BSSID broadcasting that same name (the classic rogue-AP / Wi-Fi-pineapple trick), and highlights security downgrades (e.g. your WPA2 network suddenly appearing as Open). - Threat log — a scrollable, timestamped timeline of every deauth, disassoc and twin sighting. - Tunable — lock to one channel or hop all 13, pick alarm sensitivity (High / Medium / Low), toggle sound / vibration / LED independently. - Clean ESP32 protocol — a simple, debuggable UART line protocol. The Flipper is the brain + UI, the ESP32 is the radio. - Local & private — everything runs on your own hardware. No cloud, no accounts, nothing phones home.

Getting started

# one-time
python3 -m pip install --upgrade ufbt

# from the repo root, with your Flipper plugged in over USB:
ufbt            # build argus.fap into ./dist
ufbt launch     # build, upload to the Flipper and open it
Scope. Use it to monitor networks you own or are explicitly authorised to test. Passively capturing frames
