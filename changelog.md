# Changelog

## 1.0

First release.

- 13 levels, from octaves and fifths through to the tritone
- Ascending, Descending and Mixed modes, each with its own progress
- Teaching screen for every new interval, with a tune that opens with it
- Mix levels that review everything learned so far, with three lives
- Interval reference screen that plays any of the thirteen on demand
- Hints, stars, streaks and lifetime accuracy stats
- Settings for note length, fixed or random root, tune hints, vibro and LED
