## v1.0

First release.

- Five modes: CLASSIC, RULES, REFLEX, CHALLENGE and a seeded DAILY run every
  Flipper plays the same
- Seven rules that change what you press, announced before each round
- Four assists — ears, LED, shapes, arrows — each keeping its own records
- Haptic feedback that traces every note of every pattern, usable as an assist
  on its own with the sound off
- Ten points a note, a hundred a round, and no hidden multiplier
- Records per mode, time, speed and assist, with stats kept separately
