# JezzBall

Claim the arena without letting a bouncing ball hit an unfinished wall.
Complete a wall to divide the playfield; every newly enclosed region without a
ball is captured. Secure 75% of the arena before the timer expires.

## How to play

- Use the D-pad to move the builder.
- Left or right selects a horizontal wall.
- Up or down selects a vertical wall.
- Press OK to build.
- Press Back to pause or resume.
- Hold Back to quit.

A ball touching an unfinished wall destroys it and costs one life. Completed
walls are permanent.

## Features

- Six progressively harder levels
- Two-ended wall construction
- Lives, scoring, and level timers
- Pause, game-over, and victory screens
- Graphics designed for the native 128 by 64 display
